vaani is a fully digital service. There are no physical goods, so nothing is shipped and no delivery address or courier is involved.

How a purchase is delivered

When you buy a recharge pack on Pricing, the turns are credited to your vaani account automatically as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment — normally within a few seconds. The updated balance is visible on your Account page and on the translate screen.

There is no separate delivery step and no waiting period. Access to the service itself is immediate on sign-up.

If the turns don't appear

If a payment succeeded but your balance didn't update within a few minutes, contact {{ contactEmail }} with your payment details. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for how that's resolved.
