Privacy Policy

What vaani collects, and why.

Last updated 2026-08-30. vaani is an early-stage product — this page will be kept up to date as what's built changes, rather than left to drift from reality.

What we collect

  • Your email and display name, from signing in with Google or email/password (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Audio you record to translate — sent to Sarvam AI for processing (speech-to-text, translation, and text-to-speech) and not kept as audio afterward; only the resulting transcript and translated text are stored.
  • Conversation history — the transcript and translation text of each turn, kept until you delete it from the History page.
  • Usage and purchase records — your remaining translation balance and a record of any recharge packs you buy, to run the product and show you your own history.

Who else sees it

vaani is built on a small number of specific providers, each doing one job:

  • Firebase (Google) — sign-in and identity.
  • Neon — hosts the database where your account and history live.
  • Sarvam AI — processes audio for transcription, translation, and speech output.
  • Razorpay — processes payments for recharge packs; vaani never sees or stores your card/UPI details directly.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone outside of what's needed to run the product above.
