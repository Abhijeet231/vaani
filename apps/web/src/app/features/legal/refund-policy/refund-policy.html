Refund & Cancellation Policy

Recharge packs, not subscriptions.

Last updated 2026-08-30.

Nothing to cancel

vaani doesn't run subscriptions. Buying a recharge pack on Pricing is a one-time purchase — there's no recurring plan to cancel, and the turns you buy don't expire or reset on a schedule.

Refunds

Since a recharge pack is credited to your account immediately after payment and can be used right away, purchases are generally non-refundable once the turns have been credited.

If a payment was deducted but the turns never showed up in your account — a failed or incomplete transaction on our end, not a change of mind — contact {{ contactEmail }} with your payment details and we'll fix it, either by crediting the missing turns or refunding the payment through Razorpay.
