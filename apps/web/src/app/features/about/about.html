About

Language shouldn't be the reason a conversation doesn't happen.

Most translation tools assume both people are holding a phone. vaani doesn't — it runs entirely from one person's device. The other person just talks and listens, the way they would in any conversation.

How it works, without the jargon

You pick a direction — say, Hindi to Kannada — and tap to record. vaani transcribes what it hears, translates it, and shows the result instantly. Flip the direction and the other person can reply the same way, on the same phone. Tap the speaker icon and it reads the translation aloud too, so reading isn't the only option.

Under the hood it's three real, purpose-built speech models from Sarvam AI: Saaras for speech-to-text, Mayura for translation, and Bulbul for the voice you hear back. Nothing generic bolted together — each step uses a model built for exactly that job.

Where it stands today

1-1 conversations — one direction at a time, translated text and speech — are built and working. Multi-speaker rooms, where vaani follows a conversation with several people and labels who said what, are next.

No accounts, no sign-up, nothing stored — vaani is being built one working piece at a time, and this page will stay honest about what's actually done versus what's still on the way.
